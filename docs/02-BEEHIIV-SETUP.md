# beehiiv Setup — Click by Click

Verified against beehiiv pricing as of August 2026.

## Plan decision

| Plan | Cost | Cap | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch** | Free | 2,500 subs | Newsletter + website, unlimited sends, **custom domains**, recommendation network, segmentation, API (no Send API) |
| **Scale** | $43/mo | 100k | Adds **referral program, polls, automations, ad network**, webhooks, 3 seats |
| **Max** | $96/mo | 100k | Adds removing beehiiv branding, audio, RSS-to-send, 10 publications |

**Start on Launch (free).** It covers everything you need to publish and grow organically.

**Upgrade to Scale when either happens first:** you cross ~500 subscribers, or you close your first paid sponsor. At that point $43/mo is trivially covered and the referral program becomes your main growth engine.

⚠️ **Know this going in:** Launch has **no automations**, which means **no automatic welcome email**. New subscribers get beehiiv's default confirmation and nothing else. Two ways to handle it:
- Set your **welcome/confirmation page** copy to do the work — tell them when the first issue lands and to whitelist your address.
- Or upgrade to Scale early. A welcome email is the single highest-open email you will ever send, and losing it is a real cost. If money allows, I'd upgrade at launch rather than at 500.

## Step 1 — Create the publication

1. Sign up at **beehiiv.com**.
2. Publication name: **The Palmetto Beat**
3. Description: *Everything happening in Palmetto, Florida — in a five-minute read, three mornings a week.*
4. Choose the free **Launch** plan.

## Step 2 — Sender identity

Settings → Publication → Email.

- **Sender name:** `The Palmetto Beat` (not your personal name — but sign each issue personally)
- **Reply-to:** a real inbox you actually monitor. Replies are your best source of story tips and your strongest deliverability signal.
- **Physical mailing address:** required by CAN-SPAM and non-negotiable. If you don't want your home address public, **get a PO Box in Palmetto before launch.** It also looks more legitimate to sponsors.

## Step 3 — Subdomain and future custom domain

You chose to launch on the beehiiv subdomain.

- Claim **`thepalmettobeat`** as your beehiiv handle immediately, before anything else.
- **Still register `thepalmettobeat.com` today** (~$12/yr, Cloudflare or Namecheap). Do not announce the newsletter publicly until you own it. Custom domains are free on Launch, so you can point it whenever you're ready — but the name is only yours if you buy it.

## Step 4 — Signup form and landing page

Design → Website. Keep the default template, change these:

- **Headline:** Everything happening in Palmetto — in your inbox by 6 AM.
- **Subhead:** Free. Twice a week. Written by someone who actually lives here.
- **Button:** Get The Palmetto Beat
- **Under the form:** Join your neighbors in Palmetto and Ellenton. Unsubscribe anytime.

Ask for **email only.** Every extra field cuts conversion. You can collect neighborhood later via a poll once you're on Scale.

## Step 5 — Confirmation page (doing your welcome email's job)

Settings → Subscribe Flow → confirmation/success page. Write:

> **You're in.** Your first issue of The Palmetto Beat lands Tuesday at 6 AM.
>
> Two quick things: add our address to your contacts so we don't end up in Promotions — and if you know something happening in Palmetto, just hit reply. Tips from readers are how this thing works.
>
> — [Your name]

## Step 6 — Recommendation network (free, turn it on)

Grow → Recommendations. This is the best free growth lever on the Launch plan — other newsletters recommend you to their new subscribers.

- Turn it on.
- Search for and request recommendations from other **Florida and Tampa Bay area** newsletters. Local-to-local is far higher converting than generic.
- Recommend two or three complementary publications yourself — reciprocity gets you accepted.

## Step 7 — Analytics and tracking

Settings → Analytics.

- Enable click and open tracking (you need these numbers to sell sponsorships).
- Connect Google Analytics to your website if you have it.

## Step 8 — Before you send issue #1

- [ ] Send a test to yourself and open it on **your phone** — 70%+ of local newsletter reads are mobile
- [ ] Check it in Gmail specifically, and confirm it lands in Primary rather than Promotions
- [ ] Every link clicked and verified
- [ ] Unsubscribe link present and working
- [ ] Physical address present in footer
- [ ] Subject line under 45 characters
- [ ] Preview text written (not auto-filled from the first line)
- [ ] Send scheduled for **6:00 AM**, not sent manually

## Step 9 — Deliverability protection

For your first three sends, your domain has zero reputation. Protect it:

- **Send your first issue to your warmest list only** — people who personally know you and will open it. High open rates on early sends teach inbox providers you're legitimate.
- Do **not** import a purchased or scraped list. One spam-trap hit can poison your sender reputation permanently.
- Do **not** import contacts who never asked to be subscribed. Manually adding your phone contacts feels fast and is the most common way new newsletters get killed. Invite them to sign up instead.
- Ask your first 50 subscribers to reply to issue #1 with one word. Replies are the strongest positive signal there is.

## When you upgrade to Scale, do these immediately

1. **Welcome automation** — one email, sent instantly, that tells them what to expect and asks for a reply
2. **Referral program** — rewards at 3 / 10 / 25 referrals. For a local newsletter make the rewards local: a shoutout in the newsletter, a Palmetto Beat sticker, a coffee from a local shop you've partnered with
3. **Poll in every issue** — engagement metric that helps deliverability and gives you free content for the next issue
