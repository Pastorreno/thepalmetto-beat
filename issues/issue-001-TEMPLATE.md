## 1. THE LEAD

**Candidate story: downtown's six-story future**

The strongest launch lead available. The Community Redevelopment Agency approved the sale of four downtown parcels to a developer planning a six-story mixed-use building with 152 apartments.

**Report before writing:**
- Confirm current status with the CRA and City Clerk — approved, under contract, permitted?
- Developer name, project name, timeline
- Which four parcels, and what's on them now
- What happens to any existing businesses there
- Parking and traffic plan
- Next public meeting or vote date

**Structure:** what was approved → what changes downtown → what happens next and when.

**Why this lead:** it's the biggest thing happening in Palmetto, it affects everyone, and no outlet is covering it continuously. Owning this story from issue #1 establishes what The Palmetto Beat is for.
