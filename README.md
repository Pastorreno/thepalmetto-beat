# The Palmetto Beat

## Status
Parked concept repo. This project is being preserved as a future idea, not an active build.

## Concept
The Palmetto Beat is a local community media and newsletter platform for Palmetto, Florida.

The goal is to create a trusted local hub for:

- Community updates
- Local events
- Downtown Palmetto highlights
- Small business spotlights
- Church and nonprofit announcements
- Civic updates
- Family-friendly local stories
- AI-assisted newsletter production

## Future Direction
This could eventually become a simple AI-powered local news engine that collects public updates, organizes them into a clean daily or weekly digest, and sends them by email or text.

## Possible Stack
- Google Forms for submissions
- Google Sheets for content tracking
- Gemini or ChatGPT for draft summaries
- Mailchimp, Brevo, or Gmail for newsletter delivery
- Google Sites or a lightweight web app for public viewing

## Monetization Ideas
- Local business sponsorships
- Featured event listings
- Paid community announcements
- Newsletter ads
- Monthly civic/media partnership packages

## Notes
Do not delete this repository. Keep it as a strategic concept for future Palmetto-focused media, promotions, and community engagement work.
